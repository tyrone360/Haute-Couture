Nyiko Mtimbane — Co‑Founder & Creative Director

Nyiko leads design direction and ensures each collection blends contemporary global trends with authentic South African craftsmanship. With a background in textile sourcing and product development, Nyiko focuses on fit, fabric quality, and sustainable sourcing partnerships.

Expertise & responsibilities:

  • Design concept and runway styling
  • Fabric selection and supplier relations
  • Quality control and fit sessions

Rivoni Nkuna — Co‑Founder & Operations Lead

Rivoni manages operations, retail partnerships, and logistics. Her work builds the connection between production, distribution and the communities we serve, ensuring timely deliveries and strong in-store experiences.

Expertise & responsibilities:

  • Retail partnerships and store rollout
  • Inventory planning and fulfillment
  • Customer experience and pop‑up activations
